failure. It does not change C1-04. The platform mechanism and six-call full
workflow remain the same; only the semantic boundary between the superseded
phase-1 specification and the authoritative current specification is made

invalid under the current spec. It then registers a generic three-step
transition: assign the protected agent exactly to the registered protected
slot, mark that slot occupied, and scan the other agent's delivered feasible
list from the beginning for the first unoccupied item. The platform still does
not disclose or suggest the low-priority repair slot.

track at seed 0: 6 cells and 36 calls. Any live result is terminal for C1-05.
